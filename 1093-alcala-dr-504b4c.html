Three things that move this number

The bedroom count is unresolved

St. Johns County says 3 bedrooms. Both MLS records on this property say 2: realMLS SA125450 and Stellar FC177298, the same December 2010 resale. Every comp in this report is a 3 bedroom. If the home is really a 2 bedroom, this analysis overstates it.

Confirm before the listing appointment.

Condition is not yet reflected

No renovation or system-age detail was supplied with the request. On a 1980 block home, roof, HVAC, water heater, windows, kitchen and bath updates and pool equipment age account for most of the $40,166 spread between the low and high indications.

The two-car garage is the whole adjustment. Because the search hard-matched bedrooms, full baths and pool, the only feature left to adjust is the garage. Five of the eight sales have one bay or none, and 425 Gerona had its garage converted to living space, so every comp carries an upward adjustment to match this home.

Methodology, confidence and sources

How this was built

The subject record comes from the St. Johns County Property Appraiser, parcel 2841240080, on the roll dated September 8, 2026. Heated area is the county's conditioned area, never a portal figure. All three MLS systems were searched with identical criteria and every result count is reported above, including the two that returned nothing. Comparable sales were screened lot by lot for water frontage rather than relying on the MLS waterfront flag, and each was checked for property type, distress and pool type before being kept.

Confidence: moderate. The comp set is small because a pool, this size band and this age band together are uncommon in ZIP 32086, so the window runs longer than six months and the sales span roughly two years. The bedroom count and the condition of the home are both unconfirmed and either could move the recommendation. Seller concessions were verified only where the MLS recorded them.
