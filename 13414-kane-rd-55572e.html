Sinkhole history: the one item that decides this listing

  • The property appraiser's parcel record displays "Stabilization Permit Complete" and the 2025 record card is annotated "Confirmed sinkhole activity".
  • County permit 1520371, a Sinkhole / Ground Settlement Investigations permit, was issued 04/16/2026 to Eden FLRE LLC with Ahmed Said, PE of SGT Engineering as private provider. It passed final building inspection and was finaled 04/28/2026.
  • No separate repair permit carrying a construction value appears in the county file, so the public record shows an investigation that closed out, not a documented remediation scope.
  • The appraiser still carries the building at $189,158, 83% of total value. The county has not written the improvement down, which is what you would expect to see if it considered the condition open.

What to do before listing: get the engineer's report from SGT Engineering, plus any repair scope, invoices and warranty. Those documents, not the price, are what will hold this deal together through the buyer's inspection and the lender's review. Florida requires the engineer to give the owner a copy of the findings. Confirm your disclosure obligations with your broker or attorney.

How the adjustments are calibrated

Why a feature is worth what it is worth here

A bathroom in a million-dollar home is worth far more than the same bathroom in a $120,000 home. Adjustments only mean something when they are scaled to the price point. Everything here is calibrated to the JHT $250K–$500K band, which is where this home sits.

The band values used

  • Bedroom: $5,000. Full bath: $9,000. Half bath: $3,000. Garage bay: $10,000. Private pool: $20,000.
  • None of those were actually applied, because every comp matches the subject on all five. That is the screen doing its job.
  • Living area: about $110/sq ft for size differences, below the $166 average sale price per foot. The marginal value of extra space is always less than the average, because the lot, the kitchen and the mechanicals do not repeat.
  • Sinkhole history: about $10,000, measured directly from the four flagged sales in this set rather than assumed.

Why this matters for your list price

Buyers in this band are payment-sensitive and shop on price per foot, and in Spring Hill they also shop on insurability. A 2021 roof and 2022 windows are worth real money to a buyer trying to get a homeowners policy bound; a sinkhole record with no paperwork behind it is worth real money against you for exactly the same reason. Those two facts pull in opposite directions and roughly offset, which is why the recommendation lands close to the sold median rather than above or below it.

Methodology, confidence and sources

How this was built

Method

  • Subject facts taken from the Hernando County Property Appraiser 2025 final tax roll property record card and parcel record, the county Central GIS parcel layer, and the county Building Division permit file. No listing sheet was used, because this home has never been listed.
  • Comps pulled live from all three MLS systems on 08/11/2026, combined and de-duplicated by normalised street address, then every kept comp re-verified against the county parcel record and its own 2025 property record card for heated area, lot size, year built, garage area, extra features and permits.
  • Screened to the subject's exact profile: 3 bed, 2 full bath, 1,700–2,050 county heated sq ft, no private pool, 2-car garage, built 1994–2014, lot 8,500–17,500 sq ft, non-HOA Spring Hill plat. 19 candidates were dropped and each exclusion is recorded with its reason.
  • Value derived three ways and reconciled: the JHT High-3 / Overall / Low-3 price-per-foot summary on the matched sold set, a paired-sales measurement of the sinkhole discount, and a cross-check against six automated models.

Data confidence

  • High on subject facts, on every comp's heated area, lot, year built, garage and pool, and on the permit history. All of it is county record, retrieved this session.
  • High on the existence and closure of the April 2026 sinkhole investigation permit. Low on what the engineer concluded and whether remediation was installed; that is not in the public record and must come from the owner's engineering report.
  • Medium on the 3.2% sinkhole discount, which is measured from four paired sales. Directionally sound, not precise.
  • Medium on days on market and seller concessions: only 8 of 21 sold comps carried them, because the Hernando board record did not expose them in this pull. Concessions were treated as $0 where unknown.
  • Not verified: interior condition. There are no interior photos in any source. The valuation assumes average to good condition.
  • Gaps flagged: RPR was not retrievable (session not active), so six of seven AVMs are shown. iMapp property and demographic reports were not pulled; Realist and Zillow neighbourhood figures were used instead. The Hernando Tax Collector site returned HTTP 403 and the Clerk's official-records image portal did not resolve, so the certified tax figures and recorded deed data come from the property appraiser's own roll and Realist rather than from the bill and deed images.
