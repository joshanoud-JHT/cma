How the adjustments are calibrated

Why a feature is worth what it is worth here

A pool in a million-dollar home is worth far more than the same pool in a $200,000 home. Adjustments only make sense when they are scaled to the price point of the property. Everything in this analysis is calibrated to the Josh Hanoud Team's $450K–$650K band for the Ormond Beach peninsula market.

Calibrated to this market

  • Size differences: handled by converting each adjusted sale to a $/heated-sq-ft rate and scaling it to this home's 1,835 sq ft, rather than a flat per-square-foot addition, since beds and baths already match on every comp.
  • Pool: $25,000 of contributory value at this price band, per the team's calibrated adjustment table.
  • Garage bay: $15,000 per bay at this price band, also per the team's calibrated table.

Why this matters for your list price

Buyers in this price band shop on price per foot but also weigh the pool and garage heavily on the Ormond Beach peninsula. Over-improving relative to the neighborhood rarely returns dollar-for-dollar, and under-pricing leaves money on the table. The adjustments above keep the comparison fair so the recommended price reflects what a buyer will actually pay for this specific home, not an average of dissimilar ones.

Methodology, confidence and sources

How this was built

Method

  • Subject facts pulled from the Volusia County Property Appraiser (VCPA) record and the iMapp parcel report, cross-checked against the tax bill and prior deed.
  • Comps drawn from all three area MLS systems (closed sales, roughly a 180-day window given the small local sold count) and held to the subject's profile: 2-bed / 2-bath single-family, 1,300–2,400 heated sq ft, built 1936–1976, on the Ormond Beach barrier peninsula. Waterfront, condo, and mainland-submarket homes were removed by map and MLS area code, not by MLS flag alone.
  • Value derived three ways: the full 11-comp matched-set price per heated square foot, a feature-adjusted sales-comparison grid on the 7 most fully-detailed comps, and reconciliation against the 6 usable AVMs.

Data confidence

  • High on subject facts (county record + iMapp) and on the 7 comps sourced from Stellar's full listing detail (list price, DOM, pool status all confirmed).
  • Medium on the 4 realMLS-only sold comps and this section's price-per-foot contribution: pool and garage status could not be confirmed for these from realMLS's summary card view, so they feed the raw $/SF statistics but not the feature-adjustment grid. Disclosed, not silently included as if fully verified.
  • Garage bay counts for all 7 detailed comps are shown as 0 as a disclosed simplification - Stellar's grid view carries no garage column, and per-comp garage detail could not be confirmed within this run's scope.

  • All three MLS systems were searched with the same criteria, because agents outside the local board sometimes list a home in the "wrong" MLS.
