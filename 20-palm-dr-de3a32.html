How the adjustments are calibrated

Why a feature is worth what it is worth here

A bathroom in a million-dollar home is worth far more than the same bathroom in a $50,000 home. The JHT team's adjustment sheet uses a 26-band scale that picks the right dollar values for a property's estimated price range; the comp-median-based estimate here landed at roughly $348,614, which places this analysis in the $250K–$500K band.

This band's values

  • Bedroom: $5,000. Full bath: $9,000. Half bath: $3,000 (derived as full bath × 0.3, rounded up to the nearest $500).
  • Garage space: $10,000 per bay. Private pool: $20,000 of contributory value.
  • These same feature types scale all the way up to $28,000/bedroom and $135,000/pool at the $9.8M+ band; the numbers above are specific to this home's price point, not a generic table.

Why this matters for your list price

Buyers in this price band shop on layout and price per foot as much as raw size. The subject's pool is a real asset here (this comp set is pool-only, screened for it specifically), while the lack of a garage is a real drag that the adjustment model prices in rather than ignores. Keeping the comparison feature-honest is what lets the recommended price reflect what a buyer will actually pay for this specific home.

Methodology, confidence and sources

How this was built

Method

  • Subject facts pulled from the Volusia County Property Appraiser record and cross-checked against the prior MLS listing, RPR, and Zillow.
  • Comps drawn from Stellar MLS (Active + Pending + Sold, 180-day window) and held to the subject's profile: single-family, private pool, no garage, built 1935–1975, non-HOA. Oceanfront, condo, HOA, and canal-front homes were removed by map, not by MLS flag alone.
  • Value derived three ways: the JHT band-based feature-adjustment grid on the 4 kept comps, the Active/Pending/Sold bucket summary, and reconciliation against all seven AVMs.

Data confidence

  • High on subject facts (county-recorded deed, permits, tax bill) and on all 5 matched MLS listings (full listing detail pulled for each).
  • 1259 Scottsdale Dr (Sold) was deliberately excluded from every value calculation as a distressed fixer-upper outlier; it is shown in the comp table only for transparency.
  • The comp set is thin: 1 active, 1 pending, 2 kept solds. The seven-AVM cross-check (average $338,708, full range $260,500 – $384,500) is the stronger corroboration for this report given the small sold sample.
  • All three MLS systems were searched with the same criteria, because agents outside the local board sometimes list a home in the "wrong" MLS. Stellar MLS is primary for Volusia County and is the basis here (5 matches). realMLS (Flexmls) was searched via its FBS data-share (11 active + 120 historical closed for the zip); nothing matched outside Stellar's set. Citrus County MLS is a standalone system with no data-share to either and returned a genuine 0 matches, both by address and by zip alone.
  • iMapp (Stellar's separate tax/demographics add-on) could not be reached after two attempts (a known SSO limitation); its facts duplicate the county appraiser record already captured here, so nothing in this report depends on it.
