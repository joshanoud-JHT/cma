How the adjustments are calibrated

Why a feature is worth what it is worth here

A bathroom in a million-dollar home is worth far more than the same bathroom in a $50,000 home. Adjustments only make sense when they are scaled to the price point of the property. This analysis uses the JHT team's $900K–$1.25M adjustment band, selected because the raw median $/SF across the matched comps, applied to this home's 3,139 sq ft, lands just under $1M.

Calibrated to this market

  • Living area: about $150/sf for differences. That is well below the $321 average sale price per foot for the sold comps, because the marginal value of extra space is always less than the average.
  • Pool + cage: $35,000 of contributory value at this price point, per the JHT band table. Every core comp has one, so this rarely comes into play here.
  • Full bathroom: $13,500 in this band; a garage bay is $15,000. Bedroom differences run $8,000 each.

Why this matters for your list price

The comp set spans two very different micro-markets at the same size/pool/bath profile: the subject's own Largo/Clearwater area (where the core comps sold and are listed at $305–$321/sf) and the separately prestigious Belleair pocket a mile or two away (where two homes are asking $470–$540/sf and one has sat unsold for over a year). Pricing this home off the Belleair pair would badly overstate what a buyer here will actually pay.

How this was built

Method

  • Subject facts pulled from the Pinellas County Property Appraiser record (parcel 25-29-15-70200-100-0603) and cross-checked against Zillow and RPR.
  • Comps drawn from Stellar MLS (active + sold, 180-day window) and held to the subject's profile: private pool, 3–5 full baths, 2,400–3,900 sq ft single-family, 2-car garage. Data confidence

  • High on subject facts (county-recorded) and all four core comp records (MLS-verified, current active listings or closed sales).
  • Gap: the county does not publish a bedroom count for this parcel; 4 bedrooms is an estimate based on size/bath count and comparable homes, flagged for field verification.
  • Gap: Realist RealAVM could not be retrieved this session (address lookup did not resolve in the browser tool); marked "not available" rather than guessed.
  • Gap: the formal nine-document source package (GIS map PDF, iMapp report, Clerk-recorded deed image, formal tax bill PDF) was not fully assembled as standalone PDFs in this run; the underlying data from each (sales history, taxes, permits, parcel map) was captured directly from the county appraiser's own record above.
  • All three MLS systems were searched with matching criteria, because agents outside the local board sometimes list a home in the "wrong" MLS.
