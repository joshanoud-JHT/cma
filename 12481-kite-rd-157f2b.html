Handle the roof before you list

The county permit file shows no roof permit since the house was finished in 2006, so the asphalt shingle roof is original and about twenty years old. Florida carriers tighten sharply past fifteen years, and a financed buyer can hit an insurance condition that stalls the deal. Get a pre-listing roof inspection and decide on a credit, an allowance, or replacement before going live rather than negotiating it after an inspection. Everything else is current: the air handler and condenser were replaced in 2021 under permit 1415962.

The barn is a real asset, and the county undervalues it

A separate 1,200 square foot general purpose barn was built in 2005 under permit 1170053 as a detached garage. Metal frame and skin, slab on grade, concrete floor, unfinished interior, no heat or air. The county carries it at $13,103 depreciated, which is a cost figure, not a market figure. Two sales in this report show what buyers here actually pay for a detached workshop building, and the answer is a great deal more than $13,103.

Hernando County Property Appraiser building photo, February 2016.

There is no sale history to anchor to

The 2004 purchase at $85,000 bought a parcel that carried a 1980 mobile home. That home was removed and the current house was built as a replacement under permit 1160180, valued at $215,670 and finaled in April 2006. Title moved into the Schoenborn family trust by trustee deed in December 2004. The finished house has never been sold and has never been listed for sale. Every number in this report therefore rests on matched comparable sales rather than on the property's own history.

Automated estimates (AVMs)

Every public AVM has this property's square footage wrong

This is the single most important thing to know before reading any online estimate of this home. Hernando County records the 1,200 SF barn as Building 02 and the house as Building 03. The data vendors that feed the consumer sites picked the wrong building. RPR and Zillow both publish 1,200 square feet of living area for a 2,609 square foot house. Realist's summary header shows 3,809 square feet, which is the house and the barn added together, although its building detail is correct. Held out of the averages

11419 Raschke Run, and why it is a floor rather than a comp

This is the one recent acreage sale inside the subject's own ZIP code, and Stellar does not have it. It came out of the Hernando County board through the realMLS data share, which is exactly why all three systems get searched. It closed at $335,000 on 2,740 square feet, which is $122 per square foot, far below anything else in this report.

It is not a market comparable. The listing was advertised cash or hard money and renovation loan only. The private remarks disclose that a wood destroying organism inspection found termites, with a tent fumigation quote of $4,910. The roof was around twelve years old, the pond pump was not operating, and the county parcel is 1.20 acres, not the 2.30 the listing claimed. What it establishes is the floor: a four bedroom two bath house on acreage in this ZIP, in condition bad enough to be cash-only, is worth $335,000. A well-kept 2006 block and stucco home is worth a great deal more than that, and the rest of this report shows how much more.

Methodology, confidence and sources

How this was put together

Subject facts come from the Hernando County Property Appraiser 2025 final tax roll record card for parcel key 00819109, including the building sketch areas, the extra features list and the full building permit history. Comparable sales come from Stellar MLS, realMLS through the Hernando County Association of REALTORS data share, and the Citrus County MLS, all searched on August 13, 2026. Each finalist comparable was verified individually against its own Hernando County record card for heated area, pool, garage and acreage. Automated valuations were pulled live from Realist, RPR and Zillow on the same date; the Realtor.com panel figures were entered by the agent on the request form. Coordinates for the map are Esri geocoder matches at full confidence. The aerial image is Esri World Imagery.

Confidence

Moderate to good on the price per square foot work, which rests on seven county-verified local sales. Lower on the two largest single adjustments, the acreage premium and the barn, because this market produces very few acreage sales of this size and configuration to measure against. The roof deduction is an estimate of market reaction, not a bid. An interior walkthrough could move the number ten to twenty thousand dollars in either direction, since no interior photographs of this home exist anywhere. It has never been listed.

Things to confirm

  • Roof age and condition, and whether the current carrier will renew.
  • Well and septic condition and service history.
  • Whether the Collateral Analytics figure of $296,000 on the request form was entered correctly.
  • The tax picture for a buyer. The current owners hold homestead plus the additional homestead plus a low income senior exemption, which is why the 2025 bill is only $2,412 on a $497,812 just value. None of that transfers. A buyer at $525,000 should budget for a materially larger tax bill.
